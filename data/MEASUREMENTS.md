# Measurements

## Memory model and projections

RTX PRO 4500, engine `18f4efd9`, synthetic greedy text, 128 generated tokens: PyTorch allocation peaks exclude CUDA context and non-PyTorch allocations. The original measurement labels these memory values GB (decimal). Between 8,192 and 32,768 tokens the measured Q8 increase was 0.428 GB / 24,576 tokens (17.415 KB/token); FP16 increased 0.805 GB (32.756 KB/token). These are two-point estimates, not long-context validation.

| Branch | KV | Pool | After load GB | Load peak GB | Generation peak GB | Prefill tok/s | Decode tok/s |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3.3bpw | Q8 | 8,192 | 12.72 | 13.40 | 12.97 | 289 | 16.9 |
| 3.3bpw | Q8 | 32,768 | 13.14 | 13.40 | 13.40 | 669 | 13.0 |
| 4.0bpw | Q8 | 8,192 | 15.01 | 15.70 | 15.27 | 302 | 19.2 |
| 4.0bpw | Q8 | 32,768 | 15.44 | 15.70 | 15.69 | 729 | 14.4 |

Projection: extrapolate from the 8,192-token Q8 generation peak using 0.428 GB / 24,576 tokens, convert decimal GB to GiB, then add 0.65 GiB for non-PyTorch allocations. Keep projected device peak at or below the corresponding measured FP16 edge device peak: 15.15, 22.58 and 29.32 GiB for 16, 24 and 32 GB without desktop, respectively; subtract 1 GiB for desktop. This preserves the measured FP16 edge headroom, including the original 0.3 GiB allocator allowance. Check that the load transient plus the 0.65 GiB allowance also fits. For DGX Spark, the 1,048,576-token FP16 4.75bpw check left 59.3 GiB host memory available; the 4.0bpw Q8 maximum is a projection, not a measured run. Spark process and desktop device memory are different metrics and are not combined in the calculation.
